Features

Built for working
codebases.

Understand your code. Improve it carefully. Every change waits for your approval before it lands.

01

Ask mode

Explore your repo and get clear answers — no files changed.

02

Agent mode

Describe a fix. Kairo plans, proposes edits, and waits for you to approve.

03

You're in control

Confirm before the first edit. Approve, reject, or take it step by step.

04

Verify as you go

After changes, Kairo can run checks so you know what landed and what still needs you.
